# Disclaimers, Provenance and Liability

**Built with Claude Opus 4.8. This is an index, not a medical resource.**

## What this is

This directory is a *derived index* of case reports published by the PaJR /
user-driven healthcare community. It records, for each report, only: the public URL,
a SHA-256 of the article text as read, the date of reading, and clinical vocabulary
derived from it. **It stores and reproduces no patient narrative, no image, and no
identifiable data.**

Per the PaJR Terms &sect;5, health data on the platform is deidentified and made
publicly available in anonymised form for research. This index operates strictly on
that already-published, already-deidentified layer. It performs its own additional
identifier screening on top (removing names, contacts, dates and digit-bearing tokens
before any term is derived), but it does not and cannot re-identify anyone, and it
adds no data of its own.

## No medical advice

Nothing here is medical advice, diagnosis, or a clinical opinion. It does not create a
doctor-patient relationship. In an emergency call 112 (India) or 999 (UK). Any
AI-assisted feature merely opens a conversation against a third-party assistant of the
reader's choosing; per the PaJR Terms &sect;9, LLM output can be wrong (hallucination,
misinterpretation) and must be verified against the primary record and with qualified
professionals.

## Consent, minting and takedown

- **Nothing here has been minted.** Proposed metadata records are drafts for the
  authors; the intent to publish or mint a DOI is theirs alone.
- **Nothing is published without a recorded consent string** naming who agreed, when,
  and to what.
- **Takedown is immediate.** Any author, patient or advocate may request removal of any
  entry by opening an issue on the repository or contacting PaJR Health; the entry and
  its derived terms will be removed without question.
- Individuals may withdraw consent at any time via the PaJR consent process; this index
  honours any such withdrawal on notification.
